How It Works

Why this exists

Bike and scooter theft is common at UMD, but official crime logs list it under general "theft," so students can't see where or when it happens. Many thefts also go unreported. TerpBikeTrack collects student reports so the campus can finally see the pattern.

What happens to your report

  1. You submit it. Location, time window and vehicle type are required. Everything else is optional.
  2. It gets reviewed. An AI check looks for problems like a location off campus, an impossible time, or a duplicate report. Anything it flags is reviewed by a person on our team.
  3. It goes on the map. Approved reports appear on the campus map without your name.
  4. It's shared with UMPD. Reports are sent to campus police in a sorted export to help with recovery.
  5. It shapes the monthly report. Each month we publish a summary of hotspots, times, and which locks failed.

In this prototype, steps 2–5 are not built yet.

Your privacy

  • Your name is never shown on the map or in public reports.
  • Your name is only included in reports sent to UMPD, so they can follow up with you.
  • Your name is removed before any report is checked by AI.

Rules for reports

  • The theft must have happened on the UMD College Park campus.
  • Report within 2 weeks of discovering the theft.
  • One report per stolen vehicle.

Protect your ride

  • Use a U-lock instead of a cable lock, and lock the frame and a wheel to the rack.
  • Write down your serial number now (usually under the bottom bracket) and take a photo of your bike.
  • Park in busy, well-lit areas when you can.

Emergencies

If a theft is happening right now or you are in danger, call 911. TerpBikeTrack is not an emergency service.
